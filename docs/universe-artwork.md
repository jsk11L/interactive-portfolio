# Universe artwork

Six original scene illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and inspected before integration. They are interpretive illustrations, not extracted screenshots or original promotional assets. Files are self-hosted WebP derivatives; HTML text and menu controls are rendered separately.

## kh2-title

Saved asset: `frontend/public/images/artwork/kh2-title.webp`

Final prompt:

> Original hand-drawn watercolor Japanese fantasy RPG title-screen illustration, 16:9 landscape. Pure near-white paper background across LEFT 60 percent, subtle light-blue shoreline along lower fifth. On RIGHT third, a clearly ADULT 25-year-old male traveler with spiky brown hair in profile facing left, a black short-sleeve hooded jacket with silver zippers and yellow trim, black shorts, standing in pale shallow water and holding a blue popsicle. Entire full-body figure visible. Delicate graphite outlines, refined expressive Japanese game concept-art rendering, soft watercolor shading, very wistful and quiet, much white negative space, calm sunny daylight. This is an original adult character illustration, not an existing character. No text, logo, frame, interface or typography.

## bloodborne-night

Saved asset: `frontend/public/images/artwork/bloodborne-night.webp`

Final prompt:

> Original atmospheric gothic horror environment for a developer portfolio inspired by Bloodborne and the streets of Yharnam. Wide 16:9 cinematic matte illustration. Dense Victorian gothic cathedral spires and crooked narrow stone street, a dark coat hunter silhouette seen from behind very small on RIGHT, lone gas lantern at far right casting muted amber glow through volumetric smoke. Silver huge moon partly obscured behind high towers on right. The LEFT half nearly black and spacious for HTML title. Blue-black, oxidized iron, parchment gray; extremely fine engraved architectural detail, a restrained muted blood-red accent on a doorway, fog and cobblestones. Beautiful terrifying, slow dread, high contrast chiaroscuro. No gore, no text, no logo, no UI. Architectural artistry, real atmospheric depth.

## animals-factory

Saved asset: `frontend/public/images/artwork/animals-factory.webp`

Final prompt:

> Original 1970s progressive-rock album photograph-like artwork inspired by Pink Floyd Animals. 16:9 landscape industrial panorama of an enormous weathered red-brick power station with FOUR tall slender cream chimneys like Battersea Power Station, viewed from low riverside angle, occupying lower half and middle-right of composition. Rust-red clouds and muted burnt orange dusk sky occupy upper half, distant river and rail yards in foreground. Four chimneys clearly visible. A very small pink inflatable pig drifting high between the two right chimneys, a subtle recognizable detail. Smoke is thin and faint (additional animated smoke added in code). Authentic analog grain, aged print texture, natural dramatic sky, moody amber/brown/charcoal palette, architectural photography, spectacular widescreen composition. Leave upper left sky dark and uncluttered for HTML text. No words, no branding, no frame.
